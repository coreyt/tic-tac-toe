Perfect Tic-Tac-Toe Strategy

For standard 3-by-3 tic-tac-toe, apply the first applicable rule:

1. Win: Complete three of your own marks in a row if possible.
2. Block: Occupy a square where the opponent could win on their next turn.
3. Fork: Create two distinct immediate winning threats at once.
4. Block a fork: Prevent the opponent from creating a fork. When there are
   multiple fork threats, a move that creates your own immediate threat may
   force a response, but verify that the forced response does not create a
   fork for the opponent.
5. Center: Take the center if it is empty.
6. Opposite corner: If the opponent occupies a corner and the diagonally
   opposite corner is empty, take that opposite corner.
7. Empty corner: Take an available corner.
8. Empty side: Take an available side square.

A fork creates two winning threats, so an opponent generally cannot block
both. With perfect play from both players, the game ends in a draw. These
rules do not guarantee a win against a perfect opponent.
